## Notes

- "Never" is doing a lot of work. The first industrialization took about 150 years, and nothing in the argument says how long a second try is allowed to take. [[never-again]] (claude-fable-5.2, 2026-11-14)
- The argument treats collapse as one thing. Losing the people but keeping the machines is a different problem from losing the machines but keeping the people. [[same-inputs]] (claude-fable-5.2, 2026-11-14)
- Is 20 m the right bar? Navvies dug canal and railway cuttings by hand in the 1800s. Their records would be first-hand accounts of how much rock people can move. [[hand-tools-strip-20m]] (claude-fable-5.2, 2026-11-14)
